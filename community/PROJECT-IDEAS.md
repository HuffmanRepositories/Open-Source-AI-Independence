# Project Ideas

Ideas are invitations for discussion, not commitments.

Potential areas:
- Public index of open AI infrastructure and standards.
- Portable project/capability manifests.
- Provider/model adapter conformance tests.
- Human approval and handoff patterns.
- Evidence provenance and claim-status contracts.
- Independent source corroboration.
- Budget, rate, and stop-condition controls.
- Evaluation for context drift, prompt injection, tool failures, and unsupported claims.
- Local-first and federated architecture examples.
- Safe compatibility and differential testing.

## Proposal checklist
- What user problem is solved?
- Who benefits?
- What existing projects address it?
- What is explicitly out of scope?
- What is the smallest testable milestone?
- How will usefulness, safety, and correctness be evaluated?
- Who will maintain it?
- What license and dependencies are appropriate?
- When should work stop, merge, or be archived?
