# Discussion Guidelines

- Start with the problem and desired outcome.
- Share relevant context, links, and constraints.
- Separate facts, assumptions, and opinions.
- Consider existing solutions and alternatives.
- Avoid personal attacks, spam, and unsupported claims.
- Do not share secrets, private information, or sensitive operational details.
- Keep threads focused and summarize decisions.
- Verify AI-generated research against sources.

Good discussions make it easy for newcomers to understand the question, tradeoffs, and next step.
