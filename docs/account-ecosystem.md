# Account Ecosystem

This initiative connects related work across accounts. The purpose is discovery, coordination, and integration—not duplication or assumptions about shared ownership.

| Account | Broad focus | Role |
|---|---|---|
| [HuffmanRepositories](https://github.com/HuffmanRepositories) | Public coordination and discovery | Front door, index, standards, and community |
| [HuffmanMainNode](https://github.com/HuffmanMainNode) | AI/agent experiments, OSINT, protocols, technical prototypes | Existing projects, experiments, and research |
| [PCakes303](https://github.com/PCakes303) | Agent identity/trust, ecosystem foundations, schemas, policy, integration | Reuse and extend foundational work |
| [Ryzean84](https://github.com/Ryzean84) | Technical intelligence, infrastructure analysis, OSINT | Domain expertise and research |
| [roberthuffman](https://github.com/roberthuffman) | Defensive security, threat intelligence, hardening | Security practices and threat models |

## Rules
- Link to existing repositories and credit authors.
- Do not assume licenses, governance, or maintenance are identical.
- Check ownership and permissions before cross-repository changes.
- Keep account boundaries explicit.
- New repositories must explain why existing ones are unsuitable.
- Prefer adapters, specs, and conformance tests over copied implementations.
