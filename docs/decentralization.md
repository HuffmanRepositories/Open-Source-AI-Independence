# Decentralization: Goals and Tradeoffs

Decentralization can improve autonomy, resilience, and choice, but can also increase complexity, coordination cost, security risk, and maintenance burden.

Ask: what control is being decentralized? Can users migrate or self-host? How are identity, trust, updates, abuse, and recovery handled? What data is replicated and who can access it? Does this design improve the use case? Document measurable benefits and tradeoffs instead of relying on slogans.
