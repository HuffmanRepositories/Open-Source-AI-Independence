# Community

We aim for an inclusive, practical, sustainable open-source community.

Values: respect, attribution, curiosity, clear expectations, honest project status, beginner-friendly explanations, constructive disagreement, privacy, consent, responsible disclosure, shared credit, and sustainable maintainer workload.

Start with issues and discussions. Ask questions, share existing work, propose improvements, or volunteer for a focused task. Community growth should come from usefulness and trust—not spam, inflated claims, or artificial engagement.
