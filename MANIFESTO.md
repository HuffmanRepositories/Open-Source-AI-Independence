# A Manifesto for Open AI Independence

## The future should be buildable by everyone
AI is too important to be shaped only by a small number of companies, platforms, or closed systems. People should be able to understand, inspect, adapt, connect, and replace the tools they depend on.

We believe in an ecosystem where developers can choose providers, communities can host their own infrastructure, researchers can reproduce results, users can understand limitations, and projects can cooperate without being locked into one vendor or framework.

This is not a rejection of commercial innovation. It is a commitment to choice, openness, accountability, and healthy alternatives.

## Our commitments
- **Independence over lock-in:** design for portability; make dependencies explicit.
- **Decentralization where it helps:** explore local-first, federated, and peer-to-peer designs while documenting tradeoffs.
- **Transparency over mystique:** document behavior, limitations, and failure modes.
- **Evidence over confidence:** distinguish sourced claims, inferences, assumptions, and unknowns.
- **Human agency over automation theater:** preserve review, approval, override, and escalation.
- **Interoperability over isolated islands:** connect existing tools and standards.
- **Security and privacy by design:** minimize data, protect secrets, and define threat models.
- **Community over gatekeeping:** welcome people, credit work, and explain decisions.

## What we will not promise
We will not promise perfect AI, zero hallucinations, absolute security, universal decentralization, or effortless interoperability. These are complex engineering goals with tradeoffs.

We will work toward measurable improvements: better evidence, fewer unsupported claims, clearer uncertainty, safer integrations, reproducible evaluation, more choices, and stronger human control.

## A call to builders
Bring your experience, curiosity, skepticism, and ideas. Help us identify what is already solved, what is missing, and what can be made more useful through shared standards and open collaboration.

**Our goal is not to own the future. Our goal is to make the future more accessible to everyone.**
