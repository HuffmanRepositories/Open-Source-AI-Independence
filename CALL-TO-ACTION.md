# Join the Open AI Independence Initiative

We invite developers, researchers, maintainers, designers, security practitioners, OSINT practitioners, students, and curious people to help shape an open ecosystem for independent and reliable AI infrastructure.

You do not need to agree with every idea here. Thoughtful criticism and better alternatives are valuable contributions.

## Ways to participate
- **Build:** prototypes, adapters, tools, tests, integrations, and documentation.
- **Research:** standards, architecture, threat models, reliability, decentralization, and existing projects.
- **Improve:** report bugs, clarify docs, test assumptions, and propose small fixes.
- **Design:** make coordination, approval, and evidence workflows usable.
- **Connect:** introduce relevant open-source projects and maintainers.
- **Teach:** tutorials, examples, onboarding, and beginner-friendly issues.
- **Challenge:** identify risks, duplication, unrealistic claims, or better approaches.

## First steps
1. Read the [README](README.md) and [manifesto](MANIFESTO.md).
2. Review [first contributions](community/FIRST-CONTRIBUTIONS.md).
3. Browse [project ideas](community/PROJECT-IDEAS.md).
4. Open an [issue](https://github.com/HuffmanRepositories/Open-Source-AI-Independence/issues/new/choose) or join discussions.
5. Submit a focused pull request when ready.

## Tell us
- What problem are you trying to solve?
- What existing projects or standards should we build on?
- What should remain human-controlled?
- What would make this effort useful to you?
- What risks or blind spots do you see?
