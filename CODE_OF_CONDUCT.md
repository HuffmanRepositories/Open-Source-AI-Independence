# Code of Conduct

## Commitment
We aim to be welcoming, constructive, and safe for people with different backgrounds, identities, and experience levels.

## Expected
Be respectful, critique ideas rather than people, welcome beginners, give credit, acknowledge uncertainty, respect privacy and consent, and disagree constructively.

## Unacceptable
Harassment, threats, hate speech, doxxing, targeted abuse, sexualized harassment, deliberate disruption, impersonation, retaliation, or publishing credentials and sensitive data.

## Reporting
Contact repository maintainers privately through GitHub where possible. Do not post sensitive details publicly. Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially where feasible. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation to protect the community.
